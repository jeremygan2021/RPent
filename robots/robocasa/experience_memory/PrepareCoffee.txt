【Preserve valid mug placement and retreat】
Applicable when the mug has been placed under the dispenser: if public contact_check is true, avoid further mug manipulation. Retreat the open gripper until gripper_obj_far is true, then close it away from the mug. If a measured push invalidates contact_check, withdraw opposite the push before lifting.
