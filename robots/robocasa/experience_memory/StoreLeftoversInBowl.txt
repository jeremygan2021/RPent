【Load the two current leftovers into the bowl】
Applicable while either requested food is outside the bowl: identify both foods, their plates and the destination bowl from current observations and task language. Successful demonstrations used broccoli or onion alongside a chicken drumstick; these are examples, not fixed object identities. Transfer the foods separately into the same bowl, releasing each without removing the food already inside. Check visible containment before moving to bowl transfer.

【Correct incomplete containment and grasp the whole bowl】
Applicable when food rests across the bowl rim or the loaded bowl needs lifting: in a successful demonstration, the chicken drumstick remained across the rim and required a focused instruction to lift it slightly and lower it fully inside while preserving the other food. Reobserve and correct incomplete containment before carrying. Grasp the bowl rim or outer side, not a food item or nearby plate. Lift the whole bowl gently and verify that both foods remain inside; gripper closure alone is not proof of holding the bowl.

【Carry the filled bowl into the refrigerator】
Applicable after both foods are contained and the bowl is held: identify the refrigerator and current route from fresh observations. Use a focused instruction to carry the entire filled bowl, open the refrigerator if needed, place the bowl securely on a shelf, release and withdraw. Successful trajectories included navigation recovery and renewed bowl grasping; their coordinates, repeated turns and call counts are not a route to replay. After a navigation or grasp failure, reobserve bowl location, contents and held state before continuing. Stop only when the environment reports success.
